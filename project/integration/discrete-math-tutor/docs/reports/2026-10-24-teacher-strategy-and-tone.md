# Teacher strategy and tone update

2026-10-24

Implemented the approved conversational teaching direction in the shared psychology AgentLoop.

- Added a deterministic `analyze_teaching_turn` decision for help-seeking, example requests, explanatory questions, procedures and summaries. It records a strategy, evidence mode, evidence-reuse decision and whether a check is invited.
- Expanded difficulty recognition for natural phrasing such as “没搞懂，举个例子”. When an active formative question exists, these requests bypass answer grading and retain revalidated same-topic sources.
- Blended explanation mode permits plain-language explanations, derivations and clearly labelled hypothetical examples. If textbook sources are available, factual claims remain cited and checked. When no excerpt is available for a general concept, the tutor may clearly label a classroom supplement instead of stopping at “无法回答”. Requests for textbook wording, exact data, figures and results continue to require sources. No fabricated citations are allowed.
- Added a warm, patient teacher voice and guidance to address the learner's difficulty before explaining. New checks are no longer generated by default; explicit practice requests and high check-frequency preferences can still request them.
- Updated generation and quality-check prompts to distinguish a labelled supplement from a textbook claim.

Upstream prompt reference: the vendored DeepTutor has a “patient, Socratic tutor” teacher persona and a Chinese explain prompt for a “耐心专业的老师” using everyday analogies and concrete examples. Its requirement to lead every turn with a question was intentionally not adopted because the user wants less mechanical questioning. See `project/vendor/DeepTutor/deeptutor/services/persona/presets/teacher/PERSONA.md` and `project/vendor/DeepTutor/deeptutor/learning/prompts/zh.yaml`.
