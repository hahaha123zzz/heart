# Skill ownership map

Use the most specific learning request. A named skill request wins. The router remains usable alone when a sub-skill is unavailable. A person's level changes the depth and examples, not the owner. Each line gives owner phrases, then nearby requests that belong elsewhere.

- **myelinate**: "help me study," "explain this so I get it," "teach me," a bare topic with no known class, or "draw/show me a diagram, concept map or step-by-step picture" to understand a topic with no clearer context. Route when context clarifies; ordinary work delivery belongs outside Myelinate.
- **tutor**: "quiz me on today's class," "check my practice answers," "help with my biology exam," a named course or bare topic known to be in a course the learner takes, or a diagram or concept map to understand that course. Homework and other graded work belong to assignment, even if only the question is pasted; formal scored tests belong to assess.
- **teach**: "I want to learn woodworking," "teach me statistics for work," a diagram or concept map to understand a subject outside class, or continuing self-directed learning. A complete course package to run later belongs to course-project.
- **assignment**: homework, a problem set, a lab report, an essay or a coding assignment, even if the learner only pastes the question, including "look over my draft before I submit it." Coach the learner's attempt; general tutoring and ungraded practice belong to tutor or teach.
- **project**: "teach me while we build this garden" or another build-and-learn request. An ordinary build, product delivery or code change without learning intent stays outside Myelinate.
- **code**: "walk me through this repo so I can maintain it" or "help me understand this error." A request to fix, debug or change code without learning intent stays outside Myelinate.
- **assess**: "give me a scored practice exam," "grade my completed test," or a formal rubric and score. Quick drills and live class quizzing belong to tutor.
- **course-project**: "build a whole course I can run later" with syllabus, lessons, assignments and tests. A lesson now belongs to tutor or teach; a study schedule belongs to study-plan.
- **notes**: "study from my notes," "check my class notes" or an uploaded notebook. A general explanation without learner notes belongs to tutor, teach or the router.
- **lab**: "build an interactive learning simulation where I change a variable and see the result," drag parts or explore a model. A plain diagram or concept map belongs to tutor or teach; a software development or test environment is not a learning lab.
- **print**: "make a paper practice test," printable worksheet, blank diagram or PDF for hand practice. Generic PDF conversion or a screen-only quiz belongs elsewhere.
- **flashcards**: "make flashcards," "export an Anki deck" or spaced review cards. A quick live drill belongs to tutor or teach.
- **memory-hooks**: "give me a mnemonic" or "help me remember this sequence." A request to understand the mechanism belongs to tutor or teach.
- **study-plan**: "exam on Friday, what should I study?" or study blocks and review reminders. General calendar scheduling belongs outside Myelinate.
- **find-media**: "find me a video," "is there a good diagram I can use to learn this?" or an existing licensed learning figure, model or simulation from outside sources. Drawing a new teaching figure belongs to tutor or teach; stock or decorative images for websites or marketing, work diagrams and general asset sourcing belong outside Myelinate.
- **verify**: "check this lesson for accuracy" or adversarial review of learning material before release. Software code review and tests belong outside Myelinate.
